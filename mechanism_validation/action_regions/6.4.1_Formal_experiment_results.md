# 6.4.1 Formal experiment results

## Protocol

The experiment uses five prespecified instance seeds for each spatial pattern (R, C, and RC), with (N=9), (U=3), (L=3), and \(\lambda\in[0.30,0.70]\). All fixed-preference problems are solved to optimality by CPLEX. Each map is verified at every region midpoint and on both sides of every switching boundary.

## Aggregate result

The 15 instances contain 80 action regions and 65 switching boundaries. The number of action regions ranges from three to eight. Of the 50 within-instance gaps between adjacent boundaries, 18 (36.0%) are below 0.04. The minimum, median, and maximum gaps are 0.0085015791, 0.0512338357, and 0.2489714721, respectively. All 15 mappings pass the verification procedure. Total computation time is 449.11 s.

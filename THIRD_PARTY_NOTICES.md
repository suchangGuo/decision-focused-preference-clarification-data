# Third-Party Materials and Notices

## Solomon benchmark instances

The spatial patterns used to construct the Random (R), Clustered (C), and
Random-Clustered (RC) disaster-reconnaissance instances are based on selected
coordinate relationships from the Solomon R101, C101, and RC101 benchmark
instances.

Reference:

M. M. Solomon (1987), "Algorithms for the Vehicle Routing and Scheduling
Problems with Time Window Constraints," *Operations Research*, 35(2),
254-265. https://doi.org/10.1287/opre.35.2.254

The repository records the source provenance of these coordinate patterns and
provides the authors' transformations, generated task parameters, experimental
records, and derived results. Users of the coordinate patterns should cite the
original benchmark reference above.
